The place

Real Brazilian food,
just around the corner.

Braspaña began with saudade: that very specific longing for a pastel de feira pulled hot from the oil, a glass of ice-cold caldo de cana, a coxinha that tastes exactly the way it should.

Everything here is made to order, in small batches, the way it's done in Brazil. No shortcuts and nothing from the freezer — just dough, filling, and someone frying it for you when you ask.

Specialities

What comes out of the fryer

  • Pastel de feira

    Thin, crisp pastry fried to order. The classic of every Brazilian market, and big enough that you won't finish it.

  • Coxinha

    Shredded chicken wrapped in cassava dough, breaded and fried. Shaped like a teardrop, the way it should be.

  • Caldo de cana

    Sugar cane pressed to order and served properly cold. Sweet, light, and impossible to fake.

  • Tapioca recheada

    A cassava-starch flatbread with whatever filling you like. Gluten free and ready in a minute.

  • Açaí

    Thick açaí with fruit and granola. Cold, dense, with the flavour of northern Brazil.

  • Brazilian coffee

    Real coffee, served the Brazilian way: small, hot and without ceremony.
